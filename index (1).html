Heritage

Composed in quiet dignity since 1924

Founded by Adelchi Dorato in the deep stone cellars of a Tuscan castle, our house has maintained an unyielding dedication to slow perfumery. We reject the hurried demands of modern commerce, preferring the profound patience of time-honored maceration.

1924

Adelchi Dorato distills the first private batch within the castle walls.

1961

Oro Antico launches globally, defining our signature amber profile.

1998

The atelier relocates to a restored Renaissance palazzo in central Florence.

2024

Celebrating a centenary of independent craftsmanship and heritage.

The Process

Uncompromising Italian mastery

Italian Ingredients

Sourced directly from private groves in Calabria, Tuscan iris fields, and hillside citrus orchards cultivated for generations.

Twelve Weeks of Rest

Every single blend rests undisturbed in dark glass vats for exactly twelve weeks, allowing individual botanical notes to completely harmonize.

Gilded by Hand

Each heavy crystal cap is hand-finished with genuine 24-karat gold leaf by master artisans in our Florence atelier.
